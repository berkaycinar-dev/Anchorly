Why Anchorly

See your day in one place, not across three apps.

To-do lists, habit trackers and calendars usually live in separate apps. Without checking each one, it is hard to tell how your day will go. Anchorly keeps all three together and connected.

Know what to focus on each morning

The Today page shows only what concerns today: tasks dated today, undated tasks and anything overdue. Tasks set for later reach your list when their day comes.

Habits get their own place

Things you do every day, like drinking water, reading or going for a walk, stay out of your task list. You tick them off on a weekly chart on the Daily Routine page.

Look back any time

Calendar keeps each day's completion rate, and Archive keeps every task you have added along with the progress of your recurring series. Seeing what you did and what you missed makes it easier to keep going.

Task or routine?

Two different things, two separate places.

Something you do and finish is a task; a habit you keep up every day is a routine.

Done and finished

Task

It can have a date, belong to a project and, if you like, repeat every day or every week. Completed tasks stay in the Archive.

For example: Pay the bill
Shows up in: Today · Calendar · Archive · Projects
Kept up every day

Routine

It is not a job that ends but a rhythm you keep. You tick a box for each day and see how your week and month are going.

For example: Drink water
Shows up in: Daily Routine · Dashboard

Calendar

Archive

Every task you have added and your recurring series, with their progress.

Questions

Things you might be wondering.

Do I need an account?

No. Press “Open Anchorly” and you go straight into the app. You only need an account if you want to use your data on other devices too.

Where is my data stored?

Without an account it stays on your device, inside your browser. With an account it is stored in your account and shows up on your other devices. You are asked whether you want to move what you already added on this device into your account.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The app adapts its layout to phones, tablets and computers.

Is a routine the same as a repeating task?

No. A routine is a habit you tick off every day. A repeating task comes back every day or every week, and it shows up as a series in the Archive.

Can I change how it looks?

Yes. There are seven themes, three fonts, and a choice of English or Turkish.
